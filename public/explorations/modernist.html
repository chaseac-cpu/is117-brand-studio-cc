A / Modernist exploration

Measured depth.

Reference: Josef Müller-Brockmann, Beethoven concert poster, 1955. Inspected its concentric broken arcs, asymmetry, and compact aligned sans-serif type.

Translation: a consistent grid separates the message from the product; a geometric arc adds movement; a system sans-serif and repeated rules keep hierarchy direct. Simple shapes and one accent translate readily across screens and print.

Intended effect: precision and composure for the refinement-led brief, with movement suggesting exploration. Risk: may feel too impersonal or technical.
