B / Postmodernist exploration

Surface tension.

Reference: Wolfgang Weingart, Schreibkunst (The Art of Writing), 1981, LACMA. Inspected its halftone layers, stepped edges, contrasting lettering, and overlapping information strips.

Translation: a serif fashion headline meets technical sans-serif labels; offset frames and a dotted print texture create a restrained collage; an angled caption interrupts the product frame. This mixes refinement and expedition graphics instead of treating one visual language as universal.

Intended effect: individual style and discovery within the refinement-led brief. Risk: layers could distract from the plain product. Texture stays behind the shirt, away from the reading surface.
