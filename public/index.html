    Three settings / One point of view

    Your day changes.
    Your style stays.

    A few styling ideas to help you picture a plain white tee in your own day. Take what suits you.

    01 / Everyday

    Keep it easy.

    Try the tee with straight-leg jeans and your favorite sneakers. Add an open overshirt when you want another layer.

    02 / Casual dinner

    Sharpen the details.

    Pair it with tailored trousers and an open overshirt. Keep the rest of the look simple, and check the venue’s dress code.

    03 / Before or after a dive

    Back on dry land.

    Build a shore-side outfit with relaxed shorts and footwear suited to your plans. Use the appropriate dive gear for time in the water.

    Styling suggestions, not tested fit, comfort, or performance claims. Waterproofing and collar recovery remain proposed features.
